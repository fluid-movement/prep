Migrate useful desktop behavior and tests, then delete the desktop app. Human feedback stays in the agent conversation; TUI review offers approval and issue-ID copying, not rejection or changes-requested verdicts. Eval prioritizes contract compliance first; implementation quality follows. Small-change shortcuts, MCP, dedicated adapters, and distributed coordination are deferred.
