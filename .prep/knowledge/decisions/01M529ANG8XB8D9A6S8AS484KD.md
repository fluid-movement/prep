User decision, 2026-10-16. Done means the application works; tests are the programmatic check that prevents drift. Keep one project-level Definition of Done; remove issue- and ancestor-level DoD, opt-outs and their approval (220 issues: zero additions, zero opt-outs). Prep cannot verify that a check ran, so the DoD is guidance shown in the briefing and at the check step, run by the checking agent, not a completion gate. Remove policy set/adopt/retain, evidence records, exceptions and contract hashes. Completion requires what prep can check: criteria checked and the knowledge outcome (knowledge area). The DoD item about updating knowledge moves to the knowledge area. Criteria should be phrased as observable checks so the checker can verify the application works. Parked: deriving whether DoD commands passed after the last change from hook-observed commands, only if evals show checkers skipping tests.
