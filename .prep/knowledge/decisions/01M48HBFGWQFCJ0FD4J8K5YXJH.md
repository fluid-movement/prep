In the list, right/l on a parent focuses it and on any other issue opens the detail; enter always opens the detail. In the detail, tab and shift+tab move between relations, like links in a browser, and digits still switch views. Alternatives: separate keys for relations ([ and ]) which are hard to discover; a relations picker (an extra modal step).
