The user approved and ran this trial, and supplied these facts on 2026-11-11. Three real issues were handed off in separate git worktrees using `prep brief <id> --out` and only the prompt "Read .prep/local/handoff/<id>/brief.md and follow it exactly. It tells you where to write your report." Implementers and separate checkers used opencode with openai/gpt-6-luna. Each checker received `prep brief <id> --check --out`; the orchestrator imported reports with `prep handback`.

Issues: 01M5FS0Y17Q8MK6VSC08FJX1DX (hide worktrees from TUI project view, small), 01M5FS4DPG9B02CXB7PMTZQVA8 (terminal title, small), and 01M5FSBZJQMJ1VCPKARFATTCKB (fold children in list view, medium).

All implementers and checkers wrote to the named report file. Handback parsed every reported pass (3 implementer reports and 4 checker reports including one repeat) and wrote decisions, findings, logs, claim and criteria. No report was refused for format. Implementers and checkers made zero prep calls across all runs. The supplied facts do not independently establish whether any other tracker files were read. Eight preserved files, including the fold implementer retry, are in internal/cli/testdata/handoff-trial/ and are tested directly against the parser in their original roles.

The worktrees and title checkers met all criteria but reported that they could not verify the Definition-of-Done item "knowledge entries updated": the brief forbids reading .prep. Handback correctly treated Problems as a failed check and left both issues in progress; the orchestrator verified knowledge and completed them. Checker briefs must therefore leave knowledge-entry verification to the orchestrator, state that explicitly, and omit that DoD item rather than ask a checker to verify inaccessible records.

The first folding implementation left TUI snapshot goldens failing. Its checker marked criterion 6 not met; handback logged Check failed and left the issue in progress. The next implementer brief carried that failure, the implementer fixed the goldens, and the second check passed according to the trial account. The preserved second checker report still contains advisory Problems about missing dedicated interaction/reload tests and optional mouse activation; the fixture is kept unchanged, not rewritten into a problem-free handback.

The report format held. Only the knowledge-check wording needs changing before adopting hand-off guidance. No further trial or live eval was run for this implementation; the new guidance eval remains for the user.
