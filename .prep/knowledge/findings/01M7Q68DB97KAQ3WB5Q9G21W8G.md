# Cloud spike, part 2: parallel sessions, hooks and the odd events

Two more cloud rounds on 2026-11-18 (prep v0.3.0 environment). Round 1: probes A and B each logged one line on a different issue and pushed a branch. Round 2: probes C and D each tried a direct record read, investigated the odd events and wrote their answers as a finding on their branch. The two earlier findings carry the rest ("Cloud spike: one issue from push to merge").

## Answers

- **Parallel sessions**: two sessions on different issues ran at once and pushed separate branches without interference. Their `.prep` changes merged cleanly when merged one at a time. The same two branches did not merge as one octopus merge: git reported a content conflict in the generated `findings/index.md` and gave up. After the sequential merge the generated index files were not in canonical form (`prep check` warned, `prep fmt` fixed it, then clean). A hand-off must merge branches one at a time and run `prep fmt` and `prep check` after each merge.
- **PreToolUse fires in the cloud**: both probes' `cat .prep/issues/<id>/history.md` was blocked with "PreToolUse:Bash hook error: Read issue records with prep show <id>..." and the activity stream recorded `hook denied read`. The harness labels a blocking hook "hook error"; prep's own verb is `denied read`.
- **`hook skipped`**: a normal verb, not a failure. `prompt skipped` means the prompt reminder was identical to the one the session already received, so it was not sent again; `finish skipped` means the Stop hook chose not to ask its question.
- **Blank `request` event**: not reproduced in either probe. From the source it is most likely a `gap` event, which `post-model-request` writes when the payload has no usage object, shown without a value. Unconfirmed.
- **Reports must travel on the branch**: a session's final chat message does not come back with its branch. Round 1 lost its answers that way. A session must write its report into the records (`prep finding`, or `prep handback`), commit and push.

## Gaps found

- No `session-start primed` event was recorded in either round-2 session even though the SessionStart briefing was injected and visible to the agent. The session had `hook sent` for the prompt but no session-start event, and no after-tool or request events. Cause unknown; filed as a follow-up.
