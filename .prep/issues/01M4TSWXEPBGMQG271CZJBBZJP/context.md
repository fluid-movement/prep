Keep issue-resolution review separate from completion-policy approvals and knowledge-entry review. Current completion is represented by `resolution.md`; parsing, rendering and issue record discovery live in `internal/mdstore`, while transition planning and derived lifecycle state live in `internal/domain`. Add append-only resolution review and resolution history records, preserving `verification.md` and previous review records when explicitly reopening.

Review applicability should bind the full current resolution content ID and a monotonic resolution generation, plus a fresh fingerprint over the union of resolved verification work/config scopes and selected knowledge-review scopes. Require explicit repeatable `--scope` when a legacy resolution has no scopes, record the scopes and coverage limits, and make snapshot/reopen limitations visible. A change to files in those scopes invalidates the review; a later completion generation prevents old approval from reviving after reopen/re-complete cycles.

Approval requires a nonempty reason and declared `human:<name>` attribution under the local trust model; it does not require a commit or block unrelated ready work. Reopen is an explicit agent command with a reason. Archive the current done resolution and remove it from active state, but do not create a claim; the issue returns to ready, so normal claim gates still enforce staleness, policy and dependencies. Add `depends_on_approval` only as a subset of existing local `depends_on` targets to reuse current cycle/target validation. Keep ordinary dependency behavior unchanged.

The knowledge worker owns `KnowledgeReview` fields and knowledge-review persistence. Consume selected knowledge-review scopes/results through narrow glue and include them in review snapshots, but leave that worker’s structures and files untouched. No Inbox UI or cross-project uncertainty handling belongs here.
