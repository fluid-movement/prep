In `parseDecisions` (`internal/mdstore/records.go`) blank lines inside the metadata block after a heading are skipped instead of ending it. A `canonicalDecisions` function removes blank lines between a heading and its metadata lines; it replaces `fileText` for decisions.md in the load-time canonical check (`internal/mdstore/store.go`) and in `Fmt` (`internal/mdstore/write.go`), so prep check warns and prep fmt fixes. [Storage format](/conventions/storage-format.md) gets a note.
