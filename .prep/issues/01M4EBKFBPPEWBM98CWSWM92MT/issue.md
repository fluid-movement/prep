`i` went to the Issues screen from every other screen but opened the priority picker on the issue screen, so one key meant two things. Priority moves to `p`, directly and in the action menu (`a p`). The parent jump that `p` did goes: the tree always shows a parent above its children, and `o` still reaches it from the detail. The issue list's keymap says what `o` does: go to the parent, a child, a dependency or a knowledge entry.
