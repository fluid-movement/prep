Users choose how the TUI looks. The design system's palette becomes one theme among several: prep ships built-in themes and users define their own in their `.prep/config.yaml` by setting tokens (text, muted, subtle, accent, border, selection, success, warning, error, issue states and kinds) for dark and light backgrounds. Components keep reading tokens from the theme, so a theme changes every screen and the gallery at once. NO_COLOR and terminals without color support keep working.

- Built-in themes: default (today's palette), high contrast, monochrome (grays plus one accent), pastel, and the popular palettes Catppuccin, Nord and Gruvbox, each with dark and light variants where the palette has them.
- The theme choice and custom themes live in the per-user project config, so each person picks their own.
- A custom theme names a built-in or another custom theme as `base:` and overrides any subset of tokens; unset tokens come from the base. Unknown tokens, unknown bases and cycles are reported by `prep check`.
- `prep tui --gallery` stays the place to inspect every component and token, and gains a theme switch: cycling through the built-in and custom themes re-renders all components in the gallery at once, so a theme can be judged before choosing it.
- The main TUI's settings screen picks a theme live and saves the choice to `.prep/config.yaml`.
- `prep theme new <name> [--from <theme>]` writes a custom theme into `.prep/config.yaml` that lists every token with its value from the `--from` theme (default: the active one), so users can see and edit all tokens without reading the source.
