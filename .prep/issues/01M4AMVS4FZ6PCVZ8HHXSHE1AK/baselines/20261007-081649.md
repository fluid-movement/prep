Choosing a theme should not need guesswork: the user sees each theme on their own issues before keeping it.

- On the settings screen, enter (or space, t, T) on the Theme row starts a theme preview: the TUI shows the issue screen with the project's real data, and the footer becomes a theme bar naming the theme being previewed and its place in the list.
- ←/→, j/k or t/T move to the next or previous theme (built-ins, then custom themes) and re-render everything right away; nothing is saved while previewing.
- enter keeps the previewed theme (saved to the user's own config.yaml) and returns to settings; esc restores the theme from before the preview and returns to settings.
- No dialog covers the screen during the preview, so the whole screen shows the theme. A reload during the preview does not reset it.
- The gallery keeps its t/T switching for looking at every component.
