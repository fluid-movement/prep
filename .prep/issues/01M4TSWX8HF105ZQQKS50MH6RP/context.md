The shared domain gates in `internal/domain/fsm.go` now bind effective policies/requirements at readiness and validate applicable scoped reports at completion. `internal/domain/approval.go` adds explicit human-attributed exceptions, policy retention and inherited DoD opt-out approval; these are separate from later resolution review/reopening. `internal/mdstore/approval.go` stores hash-chained approvals and DoD/request revision heads; CAS protects approval/history rereads. Revision chains prevent approval revival after change-away-and-back. `internal/worktree` fingerprints scoped content and check configuration without requiring Git. CLI agents execute checks and report observations; prep validates the recorded contract, result and applicability. Remaining completion work: final checks and knowledge review, explicit policy adoption for this legacy-ready issue, actual scoped evidence, acceptance criterion 3, resolution and same-chunk commit. Legacy records stay readable without invented approvals/evidence.
