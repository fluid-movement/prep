The app does what the TUI does: issue list and tree with views and filters, issue detail with all records (markdown rendered), knowledge browsing, the agent view, transitions and edits. Keyboard use follows the TUI: stable mnemonic letter sequences, arrows only as fallback; the wheel scrolls a list's view, never its selection, and a click selects. May split into one issue per view once the scaffold exists.
