When a user asks for something in plain words ("the headline gets cut off after 80 characters, fix it", "formatPrice shows 5.5 instead of 5.50") and never mentions prep or an issue, what is the agent supposed to do with the tracker? The skill and hooks cannot be tested or improved until this is settled, because the answer decides which behavior counts as correct.

The decision to record (with `prep decide --outcome`): the expected behavior for three situations - (1) a tracked issue covers the request, (2) no issue covers it and the change is small, (3) no issue covers it and the change is substantial - and whether the answer differs when the user names an issue.
