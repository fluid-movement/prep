Installing or updating the Claude Code integration removes the `prep` marketplace and adds it again to move its pin. Removing a marketplace uninstalls every plugin installed from it, so other plugins from it, such as `token-ledger@prep`, are silently uninstalled by `prep setup`, `prep setup --refresh` and `just install`; only `prep@prep` is installed again. It happened on 2026-10-08: the ledger stopped recording after `just install`.

- Install remembers the user-scope plugins from the `prep` marketplace other than `prep@prep` before removing it, and installs them again after re-adding it.
- A plugin that cannot be installed again (the new marketplace no longer has it) is reported, without failing the prep install.
- Plugins from the marketplace at project or local scope are left alone; they are named in the report, since the remove dropped them.
