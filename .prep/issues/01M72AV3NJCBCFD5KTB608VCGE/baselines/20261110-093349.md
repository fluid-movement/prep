Decide how a decision recorded without `--scope` is found when code it covers is changed. Scope is what lets prep name a decision at the right moment (the edit-time notice 01M72ACGWREA9E1TFNGTJB0A98, the Stop question, a hand-off brief), and most decisions have none: 177 of 294 current decisions in prep, 26 of 40 in fpa-events and all 30 in daypack (checked 2026-11-10). Planning decisions are made before the files exist, and records are immutable, so a scope cannot be added later.

Options:

- Derive scope: a decision linked to an issue also applies to the files changed by that issue's completion commit. Nothing is stored, in line with "store only what cannot be derived"; it covers only decisions linked to an issue.
- Propose scope when recording: the Stop question and `prep decide` suggest `--scope` globs from the files the turn changed. Covers decisions made while coding, not planning decisions.
- Allow a later scope: a small record that adds scope to an existing decision, keeping the decision's text immutable.
- Accept the gap: unscoped decisions are found only through `prep knowledge find` and the issue they are linked to.

These can be combined. Scopes that are too broad are the opposite problem (21 prep decisions are scoped to `internal/tui`) and belong to the same decision: what a good scope is, and whether prep checks it.
