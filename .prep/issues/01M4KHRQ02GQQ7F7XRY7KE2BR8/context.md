`Inbox.build` in PrepKit (`desktop/Sources/PrepKit/Inbox.swift`) derives the items from `prep list --json`, which gains `open_questions` and `resolved_at`/`resolved_by` in `summary` (`internal/cli/read.go`); sign-off needs `prep guide` per defined issue. The Inbox screen (`InboxViews.swift`), `InboxBanner` on the detail, `Notifier` (UserNotifications, Dock badge) and the review state in UserDefaults live in the app. See [Desktop app](/components/desktop.md) and [CLI](/components/cli.md).
