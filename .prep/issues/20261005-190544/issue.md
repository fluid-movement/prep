Builds on the issue views. A filter bar accepts the same flags as prep list and narrows the current tab. Any result can be shown flat or with its ancestors. For stale issues the detail pane shows a diff of the requirement against the newest baseline. A check screen shows prep check diagnostics with their fixes, and a settings screen shows the project config.
