The TUI and its design system use Charm v1: Bubble Tea v1.3, Bubbles v1, Lip Gloss v1.1 and Glamour v1. The v2 stack (charm.land/bubbletea/v2, charm.land/lipgloss/v2, charm.land/bubbles/v2, charm.land/glamour/v2) is stable. It brings what mouse support needs: layers with IDs and hit-testing (`lipgloss.Compositor.Hit`), separate click, wheel and release messages, and the mouse mode declared per view. It also replaces the theme's `lipgloss.Renderer` and adaptive colors with the background color the program reports.

Move every Charm dependency to its latest release: the four libraries above at their newest v2 versions, plus the `charmbracelet/x` helpers prep uses directly (such as `x/ansi`). Move `internal/tui` (theme, ui components, gallery, issue views, knowledge screen, checklist for prep setup) to the new APIs without changing behavior: same screens, keys, colors in dark and light terminals, and golden snapshots. Where v2 renders a cell differently, the golden changes are reviewed and explained. Dialogs keep floating over the screen they act on.

Afterwards, check where the new APIs let the TUI code get simpler (for example our own overlay, width and truncation helpers, or adaptive colors) and either apply those simplifications or record why not.
