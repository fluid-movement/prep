Question: what does the project overview entry contain, and are OKF index.md files generated? Today index.md files are ignored and not generated, the overview links every entry, log.md is not treated as reserved and status deprecated is rejected. OKF v0.2: index.md is optional and may be generated per directory (* [Title](url) - description), the root index.md may declare okf_version, index.md and log.md are reserved, status allows draft, stable and deprecated, types are not centrally registered.
