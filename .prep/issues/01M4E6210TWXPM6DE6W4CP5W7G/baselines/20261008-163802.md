The TUI splits its screens (issue list and detail, knowledge list and entry, the Agent screen's card, usage and feed) when the terminal is wide enough and shows one pane when it is not. In a split next to an agent the person wants control: a key toggles between that adaptive split and one pane regardless of width, the screen showing the focused pane as it does when narrow. The choice is the user's, remembered in the user configuration next to mouse capture, and applies to every screen.
