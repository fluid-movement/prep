The release after 0.3.0: prep in every popular harness, and pluggable storage. The hooks proven in Claude Code come to the other popular agent harnesses, one child issue per harness. Third-party adapters (a database, a hosted service, a search engine for knowledge) plug into the issue and knowledge store ports over a published protocol, and the contract corpus proves an adapter correct. Its children are the work that must ship with it.
