## D1: Keep a --no-bootstrap escape hatch
date: 2026-10-06

prep init creates the bootstrap issues by default, as the requirement says. A --no-bootstrap flag skips them for projects that do not want a knowledge base yet and for tests; prime still reports the missing overview and prep knowledge bootstrap creates the issues later.

## D2: The bootstrap issues start open
date: 2026-10-06

The survey and its parent are created with their requirement, context and criteria but not defined, because the define step is the user's. prep guide on the survey then asks the user to agree to the requirement, which is also the moment to adjust the survey to the project.
