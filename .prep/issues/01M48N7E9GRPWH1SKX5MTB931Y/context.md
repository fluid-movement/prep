Knowledge: [Stack](/decisions/stack.md), [TUI design system](/components/tui-design-system.md), [TUI](/components/tui.md), [TUI editing and keys](/components/tui-editing.md), [Harness setup and user configuration](/components/setup.md) (`tui.RunChecklist`).

Scope: `go.mod` and every file under `internal/tui` that imports Charm or termenv: `theme/theme.go`, `ui/*.go`, `app.go`, `actions.go`, `editing.go`, `knowledge.go`, `checklist.go`, `tui.go`, `app_test.go`, `ui/ui_test.go`. Targets: `charm.land/bubbletea/v2` v2.0.10, `charm.land/lipgloss/v2` v2.0.6, `charm.land/bubbles/v2` v2.2.1, `charm.land/glamour/v2` v2.0.1, `github.com/charmbracelet/x/ansi` (latest). `github.com/muesli/termenv` goes away and `github.com/charmbracelet/colorprofile` takes its place in tests. Charm's upgrade guides (UPGRADE_GUIDE_V2.md in each module) list every rename.

Main changes:
- Theme: `theme.New(*lipgloss.Renderer)` and `CompleteAdaptiveColor` do not exist in v2. `Theme.R` (63 call sites, `t.R.NewStyle()`) becomes `lipgloss.NewStyle()`. The theme is built from `dark bool` and a `colorprofile.Profile`. The hand-picked 256- and 16-color values are kept through `lipgloss.Complete(profile)`, and the dark or light pick through `lipgloss.LightDark(dark)`.
- Programs (`Run`, `RunGallery`, `RunChecklist`): `View() tea.View` with `AltScreen` and `MouseMode` instead of program options. `Init` requests the background color. `tea.BackgroundColorMsg` and `tea.ColorProfileMsg` rebuild the theme in place and invalidate cached renders (`docKey`). Until the answer arrives, dark is assumed, so startup no longer waits for the terminal.
- Keys: `tea.KeyMsg` becomes `tea.KeyPressMsg`; `" "` becomes `"space"`; check `msg.Runes` and `msg.Type` uses. Bubbles textinput and textarea: `Width` becomes `SetWidth`, styles move into `Styles` (`DefaultStyles(isDark)`), `DefaultKeyMap()`. Viewport: `New()`, `SetWidth/SetHeight/YOffset()`. Mouse: `tea.MouseMsg` is an interface.
- Glamour: `WithAutoStyle` and `WithColorProfile` are gone. `ui.Markdown` already passes its own style config built from tokens.
- Tests build the theme with `theme.New(dark, colorprofile.TrueColor)`. Goldens must match, or any differences are explained.
