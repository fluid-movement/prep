## D1: Per-work knowledge review evidence
date: 2026-10-14

Store append-only knowledge review reports per issue, bound to the ready contract, explicit work/configuration scopes, their content fingerprint, and the current canonical entry revision. Each report is an agent-attributed assessment (updated, unchanged, or no-impact), never proof of semantic correctness. Completion requires one current report for every --docs entry or a current justified no-impact report; only reports actually used are copied into resolution.md. Old resolutions remain readable without synthesized reports.
