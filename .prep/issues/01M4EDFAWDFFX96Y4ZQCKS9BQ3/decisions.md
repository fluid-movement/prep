## D1: Three sizes, a gutter instead of a frame
date: 2026-10-09

Decided with the user after comparing other TUIs: three horizontal sizes and one row are enough; no margin around the whole screen, only a gutter for the header and footer aligned with pane text, and the blank row under the navigation only on tall terminals. Literal spaces elsewhere (separators inside components) move to the tokens as they are touched.
