The TUI feels cramped, the header navigation most. Spacing was ad hoc: three spacing tokens all set to 1 (`Gap` unused), literal paddings, and the pane inner width computed by hand in ten places.

Terminals have no pixels: Bubble Tea and Lip Gloss lay out in character cells, and a row is about two columns tall and the smallest vertical step. Other TUIs split two ways: dense dashboards (lazygit, k9s, btop) use no outer margin; Charm's apps use a two-column gutter and blank rows between header, body and help (Soft Serve also frames the app with `Margin(1, 2)`).

- A small scale in `internal/tui/theme`: `SpaceS` 1, `SpaceM` 2, `SpaceL` 4 columns, `Row` 1 row; `ui.Inner` for a pane's content width.
- No margin around the whole screen (80×24 cannot spare it); instead the header and footer sit in a `SpaceM` gutter, aligned with the text inside panes.
- A roomier header: `SpaceL` between `prep`, the back crumb's place and the screens; a blank row under the navigation from 30 rows on.
- The gallery shows the scale; goldens at 80×24, 110×28, 110×12 and a tall 110×34.
